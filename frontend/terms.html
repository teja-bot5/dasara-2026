This website is operated for the Dasara 2026 celebration organised by Sri Durga Bhavani Friends Circle, Bhavanipuram, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, India.

2. Pooja registration

Daily Pooja registrations are subject to available slots. The website displays the available capacity and registration cut-off. A registration is considered accepted only when the website confirms it successfully.

Kumkum Pooja is offered for a fee of ₹501 per registration. The payment is processed through Razorpay. A successful payment does not mean that the user should share card, UPI PIN, OTP, or other payment credentials with the committee.

Chanda is a voluntary contribution towards the Dasara celebration. Users may select an available contribution package or enter an amount permitted by the website. The contribution is confirmed only after successful payment.

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not request or store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVVs, passwords, or other payment authentication credentials on this website.

Event dates, timings, availability and other festival information may be updated by the organising committee as arrangements are finalised. Users should check the website for the latest information.
