Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Kumkum Pooja

All Kumkum Pooja payments are final and non-refundable. Cancellation requests and refund requests are not accepted after a successful payment.

2. Chanda contributions

Chanda contributions are voluntary and non-refundable. Once a Chanda payment has been successfully completed, cancellation or refund requests are not accepted.

3. Payment failures

If a payment appears unsuccessful but your bank or payment provider shows a debit, please contact us with the transaction details so that the payment status can be checked.
