Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Information we collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, family member names, Gothram and festival registration details.

2. How we use information

Information is used to process Pooja registrations, maintain festival records, communicate about registrations or event arrangements, process Chanda and Kumkum Pooja payments, and provide support.

3. Payments

Online payments are processed through Razorpay. Payment authentication information such as card credentials, UPI PINs and OTPs is handled by the payment provider and is not collected by us through this website.

4. Records and Google Sheets

Registration and contribution information may be recorded in our administrative systems, including Google Sheets, for managing the celebration and maintaining transaction records.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate registration, payment or related website services, or where required by law.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used for festival administration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
